# Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: Replace before publishing

Sentence Sprint is an offline educational Android app for sentence-building practice.

Package name: com.sunnyapps.sentencebuilder

## Information Collection

Sentence Sprint does not collect personal information.

The app does not require account creation, login, email address, name, phone number, location, camera access, microphone access, contacts access, or any similar personal information.

## Offline Functionality

The app works offline. Sentence content is stored locally inside the app.

## Local Progress Storage

The app saves game progress locally on the device, such as best scores, stars, completed levels, completed sentence counts, and attempt counts.

This progress is stored only on the device. It is not transmitted to any server.

## Advertising And Analytics

Sentence Sprint does not use advertising SDKs.

Sentence Sprint does not use analytics SDKs.

Sentence Sprint does not use third-party tracking SDKs.

## Data Sharing

No data is shared with third parties.

## Internet Access

The app does not request internet permission and does not transmit data to a server.
