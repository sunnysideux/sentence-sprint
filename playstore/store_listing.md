# Google Play Store Listing Draft

## App Title

Sentence Sprint

## Short Description

Build simple English sentences with fun word-card puzzles.

## Full Description

Sentence Sprint is a child-friendly educational puzzle app for school-age learners.

Learners build English sentences by arranging word-group cards in the correct order. Instead of splitting every sentence into single words, the game uses meaningful chunks such as "The little boy", "plays", and "football". This keeps the activity simple, readable, and friendly for young learners.

The app includes five levels:

- Simple Sentences
- Everyday Sentences
- Places and Prepositions
- Daily Routine Sentences
- Science Sentences

Each level contains offline sentence puzzles. Learners tap cards into empty slots, check their answer, use hints when stuck, and earn stars after completing a level.

Features:

- Word-group sentence puzzles
- Five progressive levels
- 150 offline sentence items
- Gentle feedback and child-friendly language
- TextToSpeech support for sentence reading
- Local progress saving on the device
- Smooth lightweight animations
- No ads
- No login
- No personal data collection
- Works offline

Sentence Sprint is designed for calm, focused practice. It does not include social features, external links, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking.
