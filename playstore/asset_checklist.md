# Store Asset Checklist

Generated or included in this project:

- Adaptive launcher icon resources
- Original vector foreground icon
- `play_store_icon_512.png`
- `feature_graphic_1024x500.png`
- Draft store listing text
- Draft privacy policy
- GitHub Pages-ready `privacy_policy.html`
- Data safety notes
- Play Console submission checklist

Recommended final Google Play assets:

- 512 x 512 Play Store icon PNG with no manually rounded corners
- 1024 x 500 feature graphic PNG
- At least 2 phone screenshots
- At least 1 tablet screenshot if tablet support is marketed

Suggested phone screenshots:

- Home screen
- Level selection screen
- Game screen with word cards and slots
- Result screen with stars
- Progress screen

Suggested tablet screenshots:

- Game screen in landscape or large portrait layout
- Level selection screen showing multiple level cards

Screenshot guidance:

- Use real app screens from a debug or release build.
- Avoid device frames unless Play Console requirements allow them.
- Do not add unsupported claims such as "best", "#1", "guaranteed", or "free forever".
- Keep child-facing screenshots calm and age-appropriate.

Before publishing, capture fresh screenshots from the release or internal testing build so they reflect the final package and Privacy & Legal screen.
